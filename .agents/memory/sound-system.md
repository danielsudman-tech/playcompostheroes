---
name: Sound UX
description: Product decision about background sound in Compost Heroes.
---

Keep sound as brief, meaningful gameplay cues with a user-controlled mute switch. Do not restart a continuous ambient oscillator or hum during active play.

**Why:** Play-test participants found an always-on low tone distracting and interpreted it as a device problem. Short state-change cues still help without competing with spoken facilitation over Zoom.

**How to apply:** Any future sound changes should preserve active-player emphasis and quieter cues for others, but should not run a continuous audio bed.

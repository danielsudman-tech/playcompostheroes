---
name: Account deletion scope
description: The agreed boundary for administrator account removal versus historical game data.
---

Deleting a registered account removes its sign-in sessions, but does not erase historical games, analytics, or game-seat links. The admin confirmation must make this distinction explicit.

**Why:** The user asked for deleting users from the backend panel, not purging all gameplay history; existing game records may still be needed for participants and reporting. A deleted facilitator cannot use host-only controls because those require a current approved account.

**How to apply:** When changing account deletion or retention behavior, distinguish account access removal from a separate personal-data erasure workflow. Do not claim this operation removes all historical data.
